# Safety

HA ARPAV River Level is an informational Home Assistant integration. It is not an official warning, emergency-alerting or civil-protection service.

ARPAV observations and ARPAV-published hydrometric thresholds are displayed as source data and context. They must not be interpreted as an official warning level or emergency decision.

For authoritative hydrological and hydraulic alerts in Veneto, consult Regione del Veneto / Protezione Civile and official ARPAV channels.

Do not rely on this integration as the sole source for emergency or life-safety decisions.
